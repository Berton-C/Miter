This design keeps the SWI-Prolog bridge strictly at the transport and identity layer: it authenticates, maps, deduplicates, and forwards typed surface events and effects without performing cognition. Authorization is the first gate, ensuring no unauthenticated data influences downstream state. Stable identities and idempotency keys make the system safe under retries, duplicates, and edits. Durable cursors and reconnect logic ensure continuity across network failures. Typed failures and fixed local panic provide predictable error handling. Credential isolation and core-derived memory scope prevent leakage and over-retention. Versioned rollback preserves auditability. G30 tests collectively validate that the bridge behaves as a non-cognitive membrane, enforcing all obligations without live authority or credential exposure.
