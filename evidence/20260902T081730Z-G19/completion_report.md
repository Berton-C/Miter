The canonical native scheduler suspends an eight-step ledger-investigation
ResearchRNA when human contact arrives at a completed step boundary. The
suspension holds the exact locus and remaining budget in a canonical durable
event. Contact completes first, then the same state resumes and finishes all
eight actual hash-witness steps. The deliberately non-interruptible selector
instead finishes internal work before contact and fails the priority invariant.

Idle schedules are 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.4 seconds. A later contact and a due
obligation each wake promptly and reset the interval. Idle records explicitly
deny a progress claim; there is no callable model transport in this bootstrap.

a measure of real research productivity or model latency.
