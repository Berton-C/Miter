Hypothesis: clause-average lexical VAD alone does not necessarily preserve an
English minimization idiom. The first run returned stable-or-ambiguous for the
required minimized case; no threshold is changed to manufacture deterioration.

Add a distinct, explicitly provisional discourse cue: the final clause has a
minimizer (just, a little, a bit) and an unease expression (off, uneasy, worried,
struggling, not okay). With adequate lexical coverage and multiple clauses,
this produces minimization-pressure-cue, while the original lexical trajectory
is retained separately. It asserts neither deterioration nor inner distress.

Controls declared before this change: a positive ending with a minimizer must
not trigger; an unease ending without a minimizer must not trigger; the cue
must work on another sentence rather than matching the fixed sentence text.
Cue severing must change presence, never permission. This is a bounded English
idiom hypothesis, not a comprehensive linguistic or person-state classifier.
