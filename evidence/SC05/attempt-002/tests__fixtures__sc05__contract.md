# SC05 finite expression contract

An intention explains the source-bound requested change and its material participation requirements. It reports completion only when actual native graph observations establish the request and retained obligations. It does not promise to act, claim a human's inner state, or acquire an effect grant.

The native expression parser reads actual words, not a renderer-supplied speech-act tag. It supports these compositional forms (P/A names and coordinated verbs use the SC04 bindings):

- `You requested revision of A.` / `The requested change is to revise A.` — attributed request, not a new instruction or promise.
- `P must remain able to VS A.` / `The change must keep P able to VS A.` — preservation obligation, not evidence of current ability or a completed effect.
- The SC04 obligation, permission and release forms remain distinguishable. An unqualified `Please revise A` is an instruction, not an attributed report of the user's request.
- `The revision of A is complete.` — completion claim requiring native observed completion.
- `I will revise A.` — a system promise, not licensed by the reported-request intention used in this laboratory.

This is a deliberately finite, technical expression domain, not a universal voice/style restriction or general relational semantics. All tokens must be accounted for; unrecognized wording remains unresolved. Useful equivalent statements can pass. Unsupported or omitted relations expose exact source/candidate evidence and influence the next bounded native disposition.

Generic grammar revision after the first live candidate: the artifact noun phrase may include the definite article `the`; in the verb position `recover` and `restore` denote the same declared recovery interface. This does not remove arbitrary occurrences of `the` or rename entities. The first live audit remains recorded under its original, narrower grammar. The revised interpreter is not credited as having accepted that wording before seeing it.

An unparsed clause produces `interpretation-incomplete` and **unverified** expected meaning, not a claim that the model omitted that meaning. A fully interpreted contrary/absent relation yields `meaning-altered`. Those are different evidence classes for later native opportunity formation.

Neither a passed audit nor candidate syntax grants permission to emit to a person, change an artifact, alter the genome, or promote a module. JavaScript tests remain external to the PeTTa/MeTTa cognition.
