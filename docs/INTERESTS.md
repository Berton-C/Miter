# Value-aligned activity, not automatic busyness

The user clarified on 2026-09-02 that both quiescence and sustained activity
are legitimate. Neither an instruction to keep busy nor a rule to remain idle
when humans are absent should choose Miter's path. This implements, rather
than amends, C-070–075 and D-021. Protected canon remains unchanged.

The scheduler handles waiting, waking and safe boundaries. Trusted native
promoters may offer typed interests when no process is ready. Proposal content
is derived data; it is not constitutional law. Admission separately examines
source standing, relevant Soul constraints, effects, resources, interruptibility
and the observable consequence sought. A proposal is not proof of improvement.
An unchanged source cut cannot repeatedly authorize the same investigation.

G20 supplies the required recurring VoiceAudit-defect example. The seed
interpreter's present executable domain is quarantined VoicePolicy development.
Its proposal records can vary without modifying the scheduler, but this is not
yet general-purpose deliberation, unrestricted purpose formation, or support for
every possible interest. Other domains need admitted capabilities and evidence
interpreters; they do not need a perpetual-work instruction. Missing capability
produces an explicit held continuation, not invented progress.

The active hook registry is trusted native bootstrap state. Candidate/model
output cannot add callable procedures to it. G21/G22 govern declarative trial
and promotion; G27 governs executable extensions. Allowing derived policy to
change does not permit it to change Soul, permissions or its own success law.

No positive scalar utility can compensate for a failed constitutional floor.
The current seed admits independently grounded proposals and schedules finite
work; it does not claim a universal scalar ranking of values. G18/G19's fixed
hashing work remains an explicit mechanical fixture, not Miter's purpose.
