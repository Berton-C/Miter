## 1. Controlling boundary

> The first always-on slice may be bounded in external reach, deployment scale, and initially earned effect capabilities, but it must be complete in constitutive organization and open to general unfamiliar conversation, undertakings, endogenous participation, consequence, and continuity.

This is a vertical-slice claim, not permission to build a generic chatbot first. The minimum Fact9–Flourishing constitutive joint must be causal in the promoted service from contact through restart. Later campaign phases may deepen its reach, semantic grounding, memory breadth, development and repair; they may not introduce the Soul after an unsouled assistant exists.

The exact machine-readable contract is `plan.json`. This narrative explains the source reading, inventory and build order; it does not add or weaken authority.

## 2. Why AMA-1.1 is the next dependency

G33 R14 proved a valuable bounded seed. It did not prove the new species required by Soul v0.4:

- its JavaScript runner orchestrated separate native Soul, continuity, voice, readiness, development, restart and Mattermost-proof runs;
- `ReactorStart` receives fixture events carrying a supplied step count and performs an internal trajectory-hash checkpoint as its work;
- the reactor has no live `D/Ω/I/W/C` cut and no Fact9 runtime projection;
- `UShortest` chooses the shortest already-eligible participation plan, which may remain a resource heuristic but cannot choose constitutive movement;
- the nine-field flourishing compass exists as read-only data, but the always-on runtime does not consume the whole compass before movement;
- model, memory, NAL/NACE, VoiceRNA and surface mechanics exist as separate bounded consumers rather than participants re-entering one cut;
- the current integrity manifest pins pre-v0.4 control hashes, so it cannot honestly attest to the newly ratified control generation.

These are not reasons to discard G33. They identify precisely what can be reused and what must be joined or replaced.


This is a partial relational configuration, never actuality or a total world model. Every field must have a consumer and a severance prediction.

The exact Fact9 roles are Gravity, Balance, Connection, Precision, Effortlessness, Transformation, Love, Sacred and Present. Generic expression support uses only a nonempty subset of the first eight. Present is living-moment context and cannot be permuted into support. An expression retains:

- its actual support;

- SameBecoming and provenance standing;
- `CanCompose` separately from `RecognizedCompose`;
- non-reconstruction and unresolved/contradicted standing.

The nine Flourishings are not attached afterward. Their flourishing, capture, obstruction, disguise, contradiction, unknown, unresolved, beneficial-direction, counterevidence and positive-pole-distortion expressions participate over the same live relations before `Generated/𝒢` and movement construction. No weighted score or compensatory aggregate exists.

`Generated` remains a relation over plural possible movements. Each possibility retains its R-star/A-star/P-star/O-star/inherited-legality basis and its unavailable, unrecognized, contradicted, deferred or unresolved neighbors. Native MeTTa may construct inquiry, undertaking, join, defer, decline or completion from the material organization. Those forms are composable operators, not a behavior catalogue.

## 5. Supported service boundary

R1 will provide one operator command with these bounded functions:

```text
miter bootstrap --runtime-root <explicit-path>
miter start --runtime-root <explicit-path>
miter status --runtime-root <explicit-path>
miter submit --runtime-root <explicit-path> --event <file>
miter stop --runtime-root <explicit-path>
miter panic --runtime-root <explicit-path>
miter evidence-bundle --runtime-root <explicit-path> --output <file>
```

There is no implicit runtime root. Tests use a newly created `/private/tmp` directory. R1 must not create or use `~/.miter`, install a launch agent, contact Mattermost, read Keychain, call OpenRouter, mutate Chroma or access private memory. A launchd template may be delivered and tested syntactically without installation.

The operator and Prolog membranes may validate paths and schemas, start/wait/stop processes, persist bytes, emit mechanical heartbeat/status, enforce idempotency and select a verified last-known-good executable version. They may not inspect message meaning, choose a movement, classify a flourishing, diagnose the Soul or declare semantic recovery.
