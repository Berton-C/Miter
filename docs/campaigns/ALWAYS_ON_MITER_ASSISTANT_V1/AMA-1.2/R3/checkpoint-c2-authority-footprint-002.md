possible continuations are formed, the M24 and M26.0 participants place their full
finite organizations into the same contact-bound cut.  The joint contains and
revalidates:

These are not post-hoc labels.  The pre-movement organization is carried as native
participant evidence that forms `contact-occurrence-grounding`, which is one of the
material relations required by the live continuation and its R/A/P readings.  The
post-movement certificate must match the exact movement in the encounter before
the assistant may incorporate or restore it.

inheritance.  In particular, governed interpretation use, plural scoped frames,
