# 4. Formal definitions

**D24.1 Partial frame formation.** $\kappa:G_{e,c}\rightharpoonup F$
records represented/omitted regions, context, competence and provenance.
Undefined is not false; silent totalization is forbidden.

**D24.2 Contact-answerability anchor.**
$a_{\mathcal Q}:\mathsf F_{\mathcal Q}\nrightarrow\mathsf C_{\mathcal Q}$
(or equivalent typed proarrow) is contravariant in frames and covariant
in contact growth. Lawful extension preserves old components exactly;
contact writers grow the contact side and authoritative attachment may
append one new frame row.

**D24.3 CarrierOpen / Discharge.** `CarrierOpen` means a grounded
represented live item exists. Discharge is a separate Soul judgment. No
carrier-internal closure entails living-question exhaustion.

**D24.4 Prospective attachment witness.** $\omega^+$ contains
formation/assembly events, anchor/contact spine, grounded live
continuation, domain honesty, provenance/standing transport,
consequence/interpretation accounting, preservation/loss, transformation
lineage and prospective affordance $\alpha^+$ (or
preserve-without-action). It is evidence, not a writer.

**D24.5 Lifecycle.**
`Candidate → ProbeEligible → Authoritative → Durable`. ProbeEligible
permits only bounded reversible information-seeking action.
Authoritative requires an actual prior probe plus the governing package.
Durable requires later retrospective evidence and continued
permeability. Historical attainment is append-only.

**D24.6 Derived self-generation.**
$SelfGen_{K_a}(\omega)=Reach_{internal}(Seeds(\omega),K_a)$. The witness
cannot declare its own independence cone.

**D24.7 Discrimination.** For governed $O_\chi:World\to Observation$,
`Discriminates` holds iff some inquiry-relevant pair has unequal route
images.

**D24.8 Fresh material support.** `FreshFor(c,ω,l)` requires a
materially relevant contribution after witness assembly, available by
authority, in contact support but not witness support. Later timestamp
alone is insufficient.

**D24.9 Immutable history / interpretation use.** History and prior
interpretations are immutable; new interpretations are appended
frame-indexically. `InterpretationUse(e,F,h,i,scope,A,J_S)` records
causal scoped use.

**D24.10 Loss / recovery.** `LostIn(d,ξ,e)` and `AvailableIn(d,ξ,K)` are
distinct. Only dependent `Reintroduce(d,ξ,K,K',w)` may restore
availability, using renewed contact, another attached frame, retained
side information, or a reversible route carrying the exact
distinction/context.

**D24.11 Affordance.** $\alpha^+$ is predicted reach; $\alpha^-$ records
exercised participation, returned contact and changed later
participation. $\alpha^+$ alone never establishes reorganization,
authority, durability or insight.

**D24.12 Traction / stuck.**
`Traction=(contactUptake, discrimination, framePermeability, affordanceVitality, provenanceContinuity, SoulAdmissibility)`.
No canonical scalar confers authority. Contact starvation means the
route fails to carry a relevant distinction. Frame distinction death
means contact carries it but the frame maps distinct observations to the
same effect. `Stuck` additionally requires persistent
demand/discrepancy, effect-equivalent recurrence, frame reinforcement
and non-opening reachability.

**D24.13 Non-amalgamation / joint use.** Frames may coexist without
global compatibility. Joint use requires scoped compatibility or
retained tension; local joint use never implies global gluing.

**D24.14 Writers / transactions.** Writer authority is a closed typed
family covering founding, contact/recovery, action/consequence/trace,
frame formation, lifecycle, interpretation/use, loss/reintroduction,
discharge and operational status. Renderer, evaluator, frame, witness,
capability and generic carrier methods have no implicit write authority.
Same-event commit is branch-locally atomic and prevents sequential
recommit without imposing global determinism.
