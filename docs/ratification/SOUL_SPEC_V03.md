## Autonomous repair boundary

“Completely by itself” means no person, LLM, host harness, repair candidate, or effect membrane supplies the semantic repair after injury. The Soul detects, understands, undertakes, reconstructs, compares, trials, adjudicates, and decides. A previously ratified repair class and independent protected evidence machinery prevent a candidate from self-certifying or redefining the genome, tests, judge, or writer authority. A mechanical supervisor may restore responsiveness because a frozen process cannot execute cognition; it cannot decide what the injury means or how it should be repaired.

This resolves the relevant D-11 direction for exact expressed-Soul restoration: ordinary native regeneration inside the protected repair class does not require a human intervention per incident. A change to the constitutional germline, acceptance authority, protected interpreter meaning, or the autonomous repair class remains governed by C-105 and is not disguised as repair.
