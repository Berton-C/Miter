## What this grant is for

This grant would activate the first usable Miter assistant alpha for one already-bound human on one local Mattermost channel for 72 hours. It is open to general unfamiliar conversation and sustained undertakings; it is not a fixed canary or a generic LLM chat loop.

Every admitted contact must travel through the same supported organization:

```text
stable identity and project scope
  → live D/Ω/I/W/C contact cut
  → Balance unfolding as movement
  → R/A/P legibility of that same movement
  → support-specific Fact9 and interconnected Flourishing participation
  → plural possible continuations
  → native MeTTa movement
  → bounded memory/model participation
  → VoiceRNA-certified expression
  → grant-qualified Mattermost effect
  → receipt, reply, edit, failure, or absence as consequence
  → K′ and four-plane Continuity of Mind
```

The grant supplies reach, limits, and mechanical capabilities. It does not decide what an encounter means, what Miter undertakes, which continuation becomes movement, or what Miter says. No transport, model, vector rank, configuration preference, or host-language branch receives that authority.

Permitted event classes are newly observed `posted` and `post-edited` events. The membrane may admit only:

- the exact authenticated event text;

One event is limited to 32 KiB and an authorized referenced root to 64 KiB. Oversize, malformed, ambiguous-version, cross-scope, duplicate, revoked, or expired events remain typed observations and do not enter ordinary cognition.

## Outbound effects and reconciliation

The only outbound effect is Mattermost `create-post` in the exact allowlisted channel. Each effect requires:

- a current native movement and source cut;
- a VoiceRNA-certified utterance retaining uncertainty and participant boundaries;
- the current approved grant and unexpired activation;
- a stable effect ID and Mattermost `pending_post_id`;
- durable pending intent before transport;
- confirmed receipt or explicit unknown outcome afterward.

An unknown outcome is held and reconciled against the same destination identity. It is never blindly resent. Restart, reconnect, or duplicate contact cannot replay a confirmed effect. A delivery receipt proves delivery only; human reply, edit, later contact, and other consequence must determine what changes in the next cut.

## Resource and lifetime bounds

- Lifetime: 72 hours from a separate successful activation; renewal requires a new explicit human approval.
- Maximum admitted events: 1,000.
- Maximum outbound posts: 500, with no more than 60 per hour.
- Maximum concurrent contacts: 2; maximum concurrent model calls per scope: 1.
- Local model request: at most 4,096 output tokens and 180 seconds.
- Remote model use: at most 50 calls, 8,192 output tokens each, and 300 seconds each, subject to the stricter public-safe disclosure boundary above.
- Mattermost idle poll: 2 seconds, with failure backoff capped at 30 seconds. Polling pace regulates resources; it does not choose cognition or manufacture endogenous work.

Any bound reached produces a checkpoint and stops the affected new operation. It does not silently expand authority or declare an undertaking complete.

## Persistence, privacy, and evidence

The activation command must receive an explicit absolute runtime root. Private directories are mode `0700`; private records are mode `0600`. Cursor, effect, activation, continuity, consequence, and model-request journals require durable write and fresh-process readback. Credential values are never persisted.

Operational logs have a 14-day retention bound. Contact and memory records require explicit human review at 30 days; the alpha may not silently treat them as indefinitely retained. Repository evidence is redacted and may contain hashes, counts, typed standings, timings, and non-private test material—not raw private conversations, stable private IDs, credentials, or private memories.

## Panic, revocation, expiry, crash, and rollback

- Fixed local panic immediately prohibits new effects and model/memory requests while preserving readable state.
- Human-local revocation immediately prohibits future operations under this grant.
- Expiry automatically stops new operations and creates a final checkpoint.
- Three service crashes within 60 seconds trigger crash-loop containment rather than automatic restart.
- Rollback stops the adapter, restores the last-known-good registry and implementation, and preserves trajectory, grant, candidate, failure, and effect history. External messages are not represented as undone.
- Any pending effect remains pending/unknown and must be reconciled before a later grant can send an equivalent effect.
