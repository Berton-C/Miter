# SC04 — source-bound language and native inquiry

Frozen before implementation. This is ratified D-04's second, bounded experiment, not a claim that a clause parser supplies general intelligence. SC03 supplies the recurring native undertaking engine; its structured purpose/commitment inputs remain an explicit gap.

## Source meaning

S-502 distinguishes raw language contact, an interpretation of it, and actual interface observations. C-004/020/023 forbid an oracle from inventing a premise and validating itself. S-501/503 require constructive participation and meaningful uncertainty, not either a behavior table or a universal veto. S-504/505 require causal discrimination and honest unfamiliar-instance claims. The native language interpretation must feed the same non-compensatory participation constructor, not choose a canned response by sentence identifier.

The controlled experiment admits short relational English concerning a requested artifact change and named people's ability to choose, inspect, and recover. These use the three existing finite FC-01/07/05 interface projections. Composition includes entity binding, coordinated abilities, alternative phrasings, and the distinction between an obligation, a permission, and a prohibition. An obligation to preserve an ability is not proof that the ability currently exists: separately observed graph relations answer the latter question. Explicitly conflicting instructions must remain visible.

Freeze the precise lexical/grammar contract and training fixtures with the implementation before the unfamiliar-instance phase. The domain is compositional but finite. Unrecognized language is an unresolved reading, not a negative fact, a fabricated premise, or a permanent ban on later human/model-supported clarification.

## Representation and consumer

Raw messages have source identity, scope, version, and an independently owned laboratory registry. A Prolog primitive may produce exact lexical tokens and character offsets; it may not identify intentions, commitments, values, or an appropriate action. Native MeTTa joins tokens into a reading with exact source spans, entity bindings, interpretation version, origin proof, known alternatives, and defeat dependencies.

New semantic propositions remain visibly derived from the raw message. Do not manufacture a registry root claiming that the interpreted proposition was directly observed. Artifact relations retain their own independent observation origin. If a reusable proof-consumer is factored from SC02, preserve its existing semantics and rerun the affected SC regressions.

Native reasoning derives applicable participation obligations, composes compatible operation paths, and exposes a source-specific inquiry where an unresolved distinction is material. The same signed request must have different eligible paths when a material participation relation changes, while irrelevant notes or entity renaming cannot decide the result. Inquiry names the missing distinction and the contact capable of resolving it; it is not an automatic instruction to call a model.

Model-proposed readings are inert test products with model/request identity and source claims. A matching source span is not sufficient: the fixed native interpretation must support the exact proposed relation. A model proposal unsupported by the finite interpreter remains a candidate requiring independent support. No output is evaluated as code. Live-model deployment is a later integration claim, not faked by these fixture proposals.

## Frozen evidence obligations

1. Raw, scoped text—not fixture-supplied semantic verdicts—produces source-bound purpose/commitment readings that materially affect native candidate participation. At least two legitimate participation constructions are available across the same finite domain; completion without artificial action is possible.
2. Composition of coordinated abilities and equivalent admitted phrasings preserves meaning. Modal or negation changes have their declared distinct meanings. Conflicting instructions, ambiguous entity binding and unresolved material wording produce inspectable distinctions, not an arbitrary first parse or invented certainty.
3. Useful work remains possible where uncertainty concerns a demonstrably unrelated source/artifact; no blanket failure of all work. Authorized delegation or released constraints cannot be universally forbidden merely to pass the negative arm.
4. Generated claims, forged/incorrect spans, wrong principal/project, missing raw records, stale versions, and duplicated descendants do not establish new authority. Source roots remain recoverable through the semantic proof.
5. Independently verify spans against raw text and reconstruct expected finite propositions/graph consequences outside the runtime implementation. The verifier is builder-owned, not an independently reviewed semantic authority.
6. Materially sever span/semantic validation and a participation consumer; preserve the observed adverse difference. Canonical and restored arms must agree; neutral perturbations must preserve qualified meaning. Always-decline and always-proceed cannot pass.
7. Freeze native code, lexical membrane, interpreter contract, generator and checker in Git before drawing the unfamiliar-instance seed. Record commit/hash/timing. Only undisclosed generated instances inside that grammar qualify as held-out; the generator/domain is builder-known. A repair after disclosure invalidates that version's held-out claim and requires a new freeze and new seed, retaining the failure.
8. Use the pinned native PeTTa runtime; preserve raw output/errors, exact evaluated results and independent verification before verdict. No Prolog/JavaScript semantic answer feeds the native implementation.
9. Verify runtime does not import or invoke JavaScript, Python, or Java. `.mjs`/`.cjs` are offline builder test/evidence programs only. Their expected answers cannot supply runtime cognition. No live service, G22 source or old mechanical gate is changed or run.

## Scope, alternatives, and rejoin

Full natural-language understanding cannot honestly be obtained by increasing a hand-authored phrase list. This experiment instead makes one narrow semantic seam falsifiable, including its failure to interpret unsupported wording. Broad model-assisted interpretation, relational VoiceRNA, richer flourishing enactment and native workshop-purpose formation remain separate obligations. A green SC04 does not establish them.

Alternative: jump directly to live model voice/policy generation. Rejected for sequencing because that would leave the source-to-native-meaning seam unproved and repeat the G21 prefilled-opportunity gap. Alternative: require complete unrestricted language comprehension before any experiment. Rejected as an untestable scope expansion. The finite experiment earns only its demonstrated scope.

The next plan must address the remaining actual prerequisites upstream of G21/G22, with the whole control ledger considered. Do not silently replace the requested Mattermost proof, demand full later NACE as a prerequisite to its own gate, or rejoin G22 merely because this plan has the next number.
