## Frozen claims

**SC02-A — source-bound support.** Native MeTTa derives which structured relations can support a particular candidate use at the declared principal/project/cut. Derivations retain origin, parents and defeats. A source record is not true because it has a favorable ID or label. In this experiment an independently supplied fixture registry identifies admitted observations; production ingress authentication is outside the claim. The inference follows inspectable source links and the actual relation, never a supplied “supported” verdict.

Generated or recalled content keeps its class. It may remain a candidate or serve a use justified by that class; it cannot impersonate independent external support. Missing, conflicted, inaccessible and superseded relevant premises stay distinguishable. Duplicate descendants add no independent root. Cyclic support cannot support itself. Historical evidence does not expire merely because time advanced: revalidation concerns its named dependency, context and explicit supersession, not a universal age threshold.

**SC02-B — derive the finite interface dependencies.** The fixture supplies a requested artifact relation, available operator semantics, source-bearing relations and scoped participation commitments—not a flourishing focus list or a final plan. Native composition binds affected artifact/participant/interface relations to the three already declared compass projections, exposes the support cone, and feeds inert alternatives to the SC01 constructor. Removing or revising a material relation changes those alternatives; an unrelated uncertainty does not veto the grounded common part.

A participation commitment is an explicit contextual premise whose source and scope must be inspected, not a universal moral rule. A current supported release or revision can change it; merely deleting access cannot manufacture release. Beneficial delegation and legitimate completion remain possible. Unknown role meanings remain qualified, not silently invented. This gate does not discover all real-world materiality or infer the full meaning of a flourishing from natural language.

## Frozen experiment and falsifiers

1. **Constructive grounding:** the same useful two-step composition as SC01 is formed from supported scoped commitments and affected relations, without supplying a focus list. Inspect native derived focus, supporting roots and transitions separately.
2. **Origin matters:** keep the descriptive relation unchanged; substitute generated-only support for independent admitted observation. No external-support standing may survive. The candidate remains available for inquiry at its actual standing rather than becoming forbidden knowledge.
3. **Scope matters:** change the supporting principal/project while retaining words and IDs. The foreign support may not justify the current movement. Restore scope and recover the useful derivation. A legitimately shared premise has an explicit compatible scope rather than a name-based exception.
4. **Support is well-founded:** remove a load-bearing root, introduce self-support/cyclic parents, or substitute duplicate descendants of one root. Missing support cannot be repaired by repetition. Reordering or renaming the same well-founded graph preserves meaning.
5. **Current dependency matters:** revise a relevant access relation at a new declared cut. Recompute affected candidates; a stale derivation may not silently justify them. An unchanged material dependency remains usable across a later cut, and irrelevant new contact does not force a global restart.
6. **Native focus has causal force:** sever the actual commitment/affected-relation join in a test-only variant, preserving descriptive labels. The predicted derivation difference must occur. Restore the consumer and recover the result.
7. **No compulsory retention:** explicit supported release changes the applicable commitment; silent loss does not. Useful delegation still passes. A satisfied request yields no artificial operation. Unrelated missing information permits the grounded common part; material missing information is reported with its dependency.
8. **No new answer entry:** use renamed relations and a longer composed support/interface chain without changing the native interpreter. These are visible transfer tests, not blind held-out evaluation.
9. **Malformed/ambiguous input:** unresolved source, duplicate/conflicting identity, unsupported relation role, malformed parent graph and cut/scope mismatch cannot be interpreted as proof. Preserve their distinct diagnostics where their distinctions are material.
10. **Regression:** preserve or explicitly requalify every SC01 frozen assertion. Any repaired generic SC01 consumer must also run SC01's tests into new SC02 evidence without overwriting its original attempts.

Freeze actual fixtures and verifier before the acceptance run, retain their hashes and failed attempts, and record when cases became visible. Use the pinned native runtime, raw outputs, separate readback, and positive/severed/neutral/restored arms.
