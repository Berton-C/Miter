# SC03 — sustained native undertaking engine

Frozen plan, derived from SC02 closure; no implementation claim. Machine-readable bounds: [plan.json](plan.json). [Rejoin dependency record](rejoin-dependencies.md) governs the builder's stopping assessment, not Miter's runtime decisions.

## Source meaning and intended mechanism

S-601–607 distinguish ongoing native orientation, a bounded allocation of execution, and the potentially long lifetime of an undertaking. C-070 rejects compulsory LLM inference, not recurrence. C-074 forbids recording repetition as development; it does not require a new achievement before another reasoning turn is possible. C-060/062 place meaningful selection and disposition in MeTTa, with Prolog handling mechanical transport and scheduling.

SC02 supplies finite source-bound relational cognition. SC03 must use it in a real recurring execution path, not reproduce the old step-counted hash demonstration. Each native turn reads the current declared cut, open purpose, support dependencies and available operations. It derives possibilities and a next bounded disposition. A minimal-cost choice among already eligible equivalent paths may use declared resource information, but cannot buy eligibility or silently choose a human's purpose.

Durable state records the undertaking, requested relation and its source, beneficiaries/participation commitments, unresolved questions, artifact version, candidate/support trace, pending operation identity, remaining grant and semantic versions. It must not consist only of a cursor or a prompt.

## Bounded surface and authority

Use an isolated versioned graph/artifact laboratory under the evidence directory. It is a real mutable local test artifact with independently read records; its meanings are explicitly finite. The mechanical adapter applies an exact declared graph transformation after its pre-state/version and bounded laboratory grant are bound by native construction. It does not choose goals, decide evidence standing or report semantic success.

Prediction, effect request, mechanical application receipt and native interpretation remain distinct. Native reasoning may not mint an external-observation root for its own predicted transition. A subsequent independent read of the artifact is the relevant contact at this surface. Claim exactly-once behavior only for the implemented local journal and its tested crash/replay windows.

No production registry, external service, model, source archive or G22 file is mutated. No old integrity manifest is silently extended. A separately pinned laboratory bootstrap identifies the loaded controls/interpreters/adapter, and reports mismatches explicitly.

## Recurrence, continuation and interruption

A native tail-safe driver or a mechanical Prolog trampoline repeatedly enters the native turn function. The trampoline cannot choose the purpose or semantic disposition. Empty ingress does not end the process. Native waiting retains a named wake condition; mechanical pacing is tunable and interruptible.

The undertaking spans several bounded slices. A grant boundary yields an explicit resource question/checkpoint; an authorized renewal resumes reasoning rather than resetting or pretending completion. A request already satisfied produces supported completion without artificial work. Unresolved support remains a live question rather than either successful completion or unconditional permanent silence.

A slow worker is asynchronous or equivalently interruptible. Incoming stop/contact remains observable while that worker is pending. Pending effect identity and exact result correlation survive a restart. Native re-entry checks relevant source/version dependencies; changed contact may construct a different path. An unchanged dependency does not force wholesale rediscovery.

## Frozen acceptance observations

1. A useful artifact transformation requires at least two native-selected operations and multiple driver turns with no new user prompt or synthetic continue message. Every applied operation is linked to its native derivation and independently observed result.
2. A smaller slice/grant ends incomplete, persists its question and dependencies, and resumes under a later explicit grant. A counter reaching zero never declares the undertaking completed.
3. Already-complete, no-available-operator and material-unknown cases remain distinct. No obligatory model call, canned activity or forced silence is introduced.
4. Relevant returned contact changes subsequent candidate availability. Unrelated contact preserves the grounded work. A consequence-severed variant loses that justified change; restoration recovers it.
5. Restart between operations restores the same purpose, source/proof dependencies, semantic version and pending identity. A continuity-severed variant fails exact resumption. No reapplication of an already witnessed local operation.
6. A changed semantic version or revoked local grant cannot continue under an old certificate. Preserve a recoverable checkpoint and explicit revalidation state.
7. A delayed worker does not prevent contact/stop detection. Record actual elapsed times and worker/result identity; target laboratory stop/contact observation within 500 ms with a 10 ms configured poll, clearly a test envelope rather than a constitutional constant.
8. Exercise at least 10,000 empty/ready driver turns under a finite test watchdog. Compare stack/memory samples after warm-up and at the end; demonstrate no turn-proportional retained stack. Separately measure rested readiness using nonzero pacing, with no model calls and no invented progress events.
9. Freeze test fixtures and assertions before acceptance. Preserve failures, run native/severed/neutral/restored arms, independently read graph and continuation storage, and distinguish actual from simulated surfaces in the report.
10. Re-run SC01/SC02 assertions if their consumers change; otherwise verify exact hashes and run representative integrated positive/severed cases through the new engine. Do not rerun G22 or affected historical mechanical gates until the agreed pause.
