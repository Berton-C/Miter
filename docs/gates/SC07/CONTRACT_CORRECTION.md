## Corrected bounded contract

The first corrected declarative surface is a **VoicePolicy expressive-capability module**, schema `miter-voice-realization-v2`. It supplies reusable grammatical constructions as inert data, not defect-triggered behavioral decisions. The family name VoicePolicy is retained for inherited G21/G22 traceability; the implementation meaning is revised explicitly here.

A construction consists of an identifier, a proposed meaning category and a finite token/typed-slot template. It can offer a way to express a reported request, a retained participation or an observed completion. It cannot prescribe when to act, choose a repair policy, assert the standing of its own output, assign priority, write arbitrary atoms, redefine the parser, or change Soul/acceptance. Slots bind to entities and relations in the actual native intention. A module's claimed meaning category is a proposal; the generated text must independently parse and match its actual intended source relations.

Native MeTTa:

1. Forms the intention and its source/Soul dependencies from the encounter.
2. Binds available expressive constructions to those relations, including composable groups of related commitments.
3. Interprets the resulting fragments independently, preserving unsupported or unresolved readings as such.
4. Constructs complete expression possibilities from supported fragments; no fragment can buy an omitted material relation with unrelated benefit.
5. Compares possibilities by their actual support, completeness and relevant sourced participation conditions. It can identify a unique supported construction, preserve equivalent/incomparable alternatives, or form an inquiry naming the missing means of expression.

This is not a compulsory five-stage mental loop. These are required observable dependencies of the finite implementation. No order in the generated module decides semantic preference. If several constructions have equal demonstrated standing and no relevant preference is grounded, the result is explicit alternatives—not a fabricated unique winner. Choosing actual emission is outside this experiment's authority.

All computation still uses algorithms, typed representation and inference. The prohibited substitution is a stored behavioral answer in place of situated construction. The finite grammar and three demonstrated flourishing-interface projections are explicit limits, not a general language/Soul claim.
