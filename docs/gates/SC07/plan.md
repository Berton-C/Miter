# SC07 — native source-grounded expressive construction

Frozen replacement experiment derived from SC06's failed decision-contract fidelity review and Berton's explicit correction approval. Both controls are unchanged. [CONTRACT_CORRECTION.md](CONTRACT_CORRECTION.md) defines the versioned semantic change; `plan.json` fixes paths and authority.

## Claim and actual consumer

**SC07-A:** In a finite technical-expression domain, native MeTTa binds reusable expressive constructions to source-grounded intentions, independently interprets their proposed meaning, composes complete supported possibilities and compares their actual participation. Generated list order or defect labels do not select behavior.

**SC07-B:** A native source-bound development opportunity requests one actual local-model v2 VoicePolicy expressive-capability candidate; exact provenance and trial-only quarantine survive the new interface. The candidate contributes inert constructions, not decision authority. Its actual source-bound interpretation is exercised, with no quality-improvement or promotion claim.

This is the next missing constitutive capability, before wiring development into ongoing cognition. It is not broad language understanding, all nine enactors, a complete M25/M263 implementation, general creativity, NACE, G22 trials or Mattermost.

## Proposed finite representation and mechanism

Use the already proven SC04/SC05 source-frame → native intention and independent finite grammar. Extract/reuse SC06's actual audit and evidence-family opportunity operations without loading its behavioral selector into the new entry path.

The declarative candidate contains a schema, candidate identity, purpose, unordered constructions, named trial-only write surface and no external effects. Each construction has an ID, meaning category and token/slot template. Allowed slots refer to the actual artifact, participant and a finite nonempty subset of the same participant/artifact retention relations. No arbitrary evaluation of slots or token strings is permitted. A construction's asserted category cannot certify its meaning.

Native binding yields an inspectable fragment with intention/source linkage, constructor identity, bound claims and text. Native interpretation must demonstrate exactly those claimed relations without unsupported extra meaning. A candidate that mislabels completion as a reported request, or shifts an actor/permission, is rejected for that use despite a valid outer schema. Unknown wording remains unresolved, not a fabricated harmful/beneficial reading.

Construction searches compositions over supported fragments. Each complete possibility covers the actual intended relations without unsupported additions and is re-audited as a whole. The search must not pick the first full cover and stop. Order/ID variations leave the supported possibility set equivalent. Keep bounded search resources separate from semantic completion: exhaustion returns a partial search/inquiry record, never “no possible expression” or completion by step count.

For comparison, expose actual per-relation coverage, source roots, unresolved/unsupported fragments, and relevant **source-grounded co-expression constraints** when provided. A co-expression constraint refers to actual jointly participating claims whose communication together matters in this finite fixture. It is not a generated priority number, a universal brevity preference, or a nine-value checklist. It may be represented as a scoped human-confirmed relation and validated using existing support machinery. A candidate's self-asserted constraint has no such standing. Compare compositions against that relation; the same available constructions must yield a different eligible set when the grounded relation changes. Incomparable/equivalent supported expressions remain explicit if no further grounded distinction selects among them.

This co-expression bridge is a proposed finite engineering specialization of S-501/503 and joint participation; it does not prove full M263 whole coupling. It must be tested, including its absence and irrelevant/unsupported versions, rather than smuggled in as a permanent style rule.

## Frozen discriminators

1. Construct a useful expression for an omitted material participation from source-grounded intention and available constructions, without a defect→action row. Include a complete construction whose fragment comes from a newly admitted capability, rather than only a complete supplied answer.
2. Competing candidate constructions include faithful, unsupported-completion, actor/permission-shifting and unknown-language forms. Independently interpreted meaning determines use; declared category, candidate confidence and lexical positivity cannot establish it.
3. Freeze generic operators before neutral name/ID/order and new role-group combinations. Preserve full supported possibility sets under irrelevant changes. Cases are builder-authored/frozen-operator transfer unless separately withheld; do not claim blind held-out results.
4. A sourced joint co-expression relation changes eligible construction/comparison while identical text and templates are available. Absent, irrelevant, stale, foreign-scope and generated claims cannot supply it. Sever its consumption, then restore it, to expose material participation. No global scalar tradeoff can compensate a missing required relation.
5. Sever source/intention participation, semantic verification and native composition separately. Each severing must lose useful construction or admit an actually unsupported possibility; restoration recovers it. An always-reject implementation must fail positive cases.
6. Completion, released obligations, more than one actor/artifact naming variant, unavailable constructions and unresolved source grounding remain distinct. Missing expressive capability yields the actual unexpressible grounded relation/inquiry, not another behavioral lookup or invented completion.
7. Return equivalent or incomparable alternatives explicitly. No first list position, candidate ID, defect class or silently global brevity/cost preference selects a winner. A unique supported result must be justified by its actual relational evidence.
8. At most two **new** qwen-local requests, each 1,024 output tokens and 120 seconds, only synthetic laboratory data under a persisted native intention. No service/config changes. At least one actual v2 candidate must reach valid quarantine and participate through native construction; preserve rejected outputs without builder repair or false authorship. No model call is mandatory on each cognitive cycle.
9. v1 rule tables, executable-looking payloads, foreign slots/fields, forbidden writes/effects and forged candidate lineage are rejected at the new boundary. Independently read the populated compass and protected-space canaries; only intended trial atoms may change. Native control expressions must fully reduce.
10. Record raw positive/severed/neutral/restored outputs, source/version snapshots and independent oracle checks. Verify unchanged SC04/SC05/SC06 source identities where reused and exercise integrated audit/provenance positives and failures. No affected historical gate rerun or G22 comparison/promotion.
