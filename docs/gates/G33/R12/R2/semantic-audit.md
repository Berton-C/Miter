# G33 R12 R2 semantic fidelity audit

## Source meaning

R2 carries forward one undertaking formed by the R11 source-grounded default
path. It does not invent a new purpose or translate the renderer's candidate
into authority. M24 remains visible in exact state/request/schema/model/candidate
lineage; M25 in non-compensatory v2 trial standing; M255 in the separation of
model product from native interpretation; M260 in the durable generation and
development lineage; and M263 in the joint-participation trial rather than
proper-subset substitution.

## Causal evidence

Resource order did not alter the selected identity. Removing authorization
prevented selection, and duplicating the preferred resource made selection
ambiguous. R1's absent-content consequence changed R2 by adding the exact
schema and a larger but bounded completion envelope. The returned candidate
was independently recomputed. Native consequence changed two maxima into one;
severing that consequence while retaining the candidate preserved two maxima.
Fresh-process restart retained the changed maximum without another call.

## Substitution audit

JavaScript supplied public synthetic fixtures, started processes, copied the
exact candidate for the severed arm, and independently checked evidence. It did
not select the resource, validate candidate meaning, adjudicate the trial,
compute NAL truth, revise NACE standing, or construct the later ranking.
Prolog performed Keychain/HTTPS/JSON/hash/fsync mechanics and committed only
native-staged terms. PeTTa/MeTTa performed comparison, quarantine, construction,
trial adjudication, consequence revision, and ranking.

The candidate was not edited after generation. The schema and cases were
committed before the call. Provider prose, purpose text, and declared meanings
did not bypass native consumers.

## Capability and constraint audit

Exactly one fresh R2 call was claimed. Endpoint, model, high reasoning,
deadline, capture limit, zero-data-retention provider request, and disclosure
exclusions remained fixed. The R1 claim and bytes were neither deleted nor
reused. The actual Keychain value and key-shaped tokens were absent from R12
evidence. Mattermost, Chroma, history/private memory, human output, executable
effects, registry mutation, and production promotion were absent.

## Remaining gaps

R2 does not establish general purpose formation, general flourishing
navigation, arbitrary expressive quality, production promotion, full hot
reload, final clean-start G33 integration/reporting, or whole-PoC acceptance.
Its full diagnostic reports are excessively large, and initial post-final
return propagation delayed stop acknowledgement until a fresh process. A
later repair should replace duplicated full-term projections with hash-bound
compact witnesses without weakening inspectability or causal proof.

This is a single-reviewer bounded audit over one synthetic undertaking and one
remote rendering. It is not independent replication or a general proof of the
Soul.
