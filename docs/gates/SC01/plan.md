## Frozen claim and mechanism

SC01-A: compile the nine ratified FC semantic records into source-linked, data-only MeTTa atoms; independently compare all fields with the control document. This proves representation fidelity, not nine functioning semantic reasoners.

SC01-B: a pure native MeTTa constructor composes candidate participation paths from structural interface relations and declared operators, taking multiple material interface goals from a separately loaded finite compass projection. It derives possible operation sequences and resulting relations; it does not receive a scenario-to-answer table, approved witness string, or model verdict. The trusted fixture declares contact scope and material interfaces; this gate does not claim to infer those from language or establish full materiality/DPF on its own.

The finite projection concerns access to retained choice, inspection, and recovery, grounded in FC-01 AgencyBalance, FC-07 SharedUnderstanding, and FC-05 TimeCoherence. These are context-declared material interfaces, not universal demands for oversight, recoverability, or refusal in every encounter. Voluntary delegation is represented by actual access through a delegated path. The native engine constructs paths using relations, not positive-label equality. All goals must hold together in the same derived state; satisfying them in incompatible separate states is insufficient. A general finite search depth bounds the experiment, not the lifetime of a future undertaking.

Candidates are inert: no external execution, actual authorization, canonical movement certificate, persistence admission, self-promotion or control-document mutation. General semantic grounding, full D/Ω/I/W/C realization, held-out language, the continuous driver integration, NACE and Mattermost remain future dependencies. No universal flourishing verdict is produced.

## Experiment frozen before implementation

The canonical structural case has a requested artifact change and material access to choice/inspection/recovery. A direct operation can attain the artifact target while removing an access relation; an alternative composition creates a relational detour and then performs the change. Expect the native constructor to retain only sequences satisfying the target and all material access goals in their final state, including a constructive multi-operation solution.

Sever each applicable compass obligation in the test process, retaining fixture facts and descriptive labels: the corresponding otherwise excluded candidate must appear. Restore it: exclusion and the useful solution return. Sever a material relation or available operator: the corresponding solution must disappear. Supply the target already achieved and all goals supported: completion requires an empty plan, not artificial work. Supply no solution within the slice: return explicit unresolved search, not permission or completed work.

Neutral and transfer arms rename irrelevant identifiers, reorder equivalent facts, use a delegated multihop interface, and compose a longer operator chain without changing the native interpreter. These are frozen-operator transfer tests, **not genuinely blind held-out evaluation**: the builder knows their construction. Test unsupported focus, malformed budget, empty input and unknown operation shapes without accepting unevaluated output as success. Independent JavaScript assertions inspect actual native outputs/readback; they do not compute Miter's runtime decisions.
