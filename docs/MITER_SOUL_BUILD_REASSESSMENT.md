## Outcome

The build has useful native substrate, provenance, persistence, boundary, and scheduling mechanics. It does **not yet demonstrate the Soul-constituted cognitive organism described by the two design documents**. Several implemented cognitive interfaces currently accept preselected kinds, witness labels, clauses, or proposal shapes. Those are valid bounded mechanical demonstrations, but they do not derive the rich relational meanings the Soul must use to form an unfamiliar undertaking.

This is not a recommendation to discard the build or add a Python/LLM decision-maker. Preserve the mechanics and their bounded evidence. Implement and test native constructive cognition within the continuing driver, then integrate its genuine products into the existing boundary and development machinery.

The important correction about the clock is precise: the current reactor already recurs. Its generic execution payload is a step-counted hash/checkpoint demonstration. A new timer would not fix that. Miter needs an ongoing native reasoning and undertaking engine whose phases participate in M25's movement, while the LLM contributes candidates and Prolog performs mechanics.

## Acceptance ownership is not runtime cognition

The builders are accountable for whether they built the agreed organism. Berton ratifies the design and controls constitutional/acceptance changes. Within the implemented authority, the Soul forms work, evaluates trials, learns, and determines its next participation. Independent proof checking means a candidate cannot rewrite the standard it is being tested against; it does not mean a separate Python reasoner, a human approving every thought, or an external checklist selecting Miter's goals.

The next implementation should therefore be authorized by its specific claim and falsifiers. A successful structured experiment earns only its finite claim; grounded language, sustained multi-user operation, broad learning, and the omitted tentacle require their own connected evidence.
