### 1.3 Living participation, continuity, and authority

Failure is contact, not automatic conversational termination. Preserve exact
lineage and distinguish malformed artifacts, truncation, unavailable services,
inadequate evidence, denied authority, and uncertain effects. Unknown is not
absent. A failed artifact stays unadmitted; its typed failure may nevertheless
inform the same native undertaking. The receiver must not invent a file
precondition, effect outcome, benefit, or write authority to obtain progress.

Native cognition may form corrected inquiry, observation, clarification,
reframing, partial helpful participation, deferral, or decline. Those are
possibilities to judge, not a compulsory “retry/read/ask/stop” sequence. Inquiry
needs a grounded prospect, not advance proof of success. An untried operation
is not thereby warranted. A failed route is not a failed purpose.

Exhausting a slice does not complete or abandon the undertaking, suppress
independent openings, or stop the driver. Preserve purpose, attempted routes,
unknowns, unresolved effects, dependency versions, and grounds for renewed
participation. New contact, available interfaces, due obligations, native
reframing, or genuinely renewed resources may reopen it without another human
message. Neither endless unchanged calls nor permanent uncertainty holding is
development. Quiescence is lawful readiness. Another beneficial direction does
not retrospectively fulfill the original request.

Use one native recovery consumer across contact families. Membranes can repair
factual contracts but cannot prescribe semantic policy. Distinguish receiving
evidence, forming alternatives, judging, enacting, integrating consequence,
and preserving unfinished purpose: proving one does not prove all six.

Continuity has four supporting planes: append-only trajectory, exact scoped
capsules, rebuildable Chroma retrieval, and recoverable raw artifacts. Preserve
relationship, undertaking, artifact/version, attention/RNA, alternatives,
authority, learned change, and consequence—not just transcript similarity.
Retrieval distance and result order are not relevance or memory authority.
Verify exact scope/source/capsule bindings; correction is additive; stale or
missing material stays explicit. Do not replay terminal contacts or uncertain
effects. Reconcile exact pending delivery before any new execution.

PLN, NARS/NAL, NACE and VAD retain qualified namespaces, actual premises and
truth/uncertainty semantics; separate spaces alone do not prevent compiled
operator collisions. Correlated agreement is not independent evidence. NACE
records expectation/pending change before independent consequence and must
alter later participation. VAD carries coverage, source and trajectory cues,
not person-state, permission, SNS/PNS, or positivity verdicts. Its licensed
lexicon remains private and outside Git, checkpoints, and model payloads.

Miter's dedicated-user open growth environment is not a builder-curated site,
command, purpose, or skill cage. Informational and reversible owned-workspace
participation can occur under standing authority. Other principals' privacy,
named credentials, commitments, spending, external messaging/publication, and
difficult-to-reverse effects retain their actual explicit authority boundaries.
Capability does not create permission. A candidate cannot certify or activate
itself, change germline or tests, or silently inherit the human's account.
Independent trial, compatibility, activation, invocation, consequence,
replacement, state migration and rollback remain distinct workshop stages.

## 4. Working discipline and tracking integrity

### 4.1 Understand, weave, embody, carry

At stage opening, during extended work, near completion, and before **and after**
commit, verify exact control identities and reread this active map plus the
applicable full controlling clauses, meaning and falsifiers. Inspect history
and the actual consumer before fixes.
Unchanged controls already read remain context; changed controls require reading
their changes and dependent meaning. Never claim reading from hashes alone.

Name the hypothesis and expected learning **before** each small reversible
probe. Change one causal variable where possible; isolate coupled changes with
an explicit reason. Preserve raw results before interpretation and independently
read resulting state. Freeze expectations before runtime implementation; don't
weaken a failing test. Fixtures and model answers cannot solely judge their own
alleged cognition. Mechanical verification and source-grounded fidelity review
are separate judgments, neither silently supplied by an invented reviewer.

Use existing consumers before adding machinery. Each stage must produce an
integrated capacity or knowledge that changes the evidenced route. Persist the
cause, what Miter itself can now do, remaining builder intervention, exact
non-claims and next falsifier in a concise Atlas update/commit. Do not append a
second chronology to this map. A stage closes only when its claim is proved or
truthfully failed/blocked and its next dependency is named. One active stage;
an evidence-reopened predecessor is repaired without creating a side campaign.

### 4.2 Tracking integrity

**Tracking is reconstructed from primary state, never recursively trusted from
the previous tracking section.** The controls define the destination, the Atlas
maps present implementation/evidence, and replies expose the active position.
No tracker may amend the other two. This is builder discipline, not Miter's
cognition or an additional runtime controller.

At context recovery or stage resumption:

1. Read this map and applicable full source clauses; inspect current branch,
   revision and changes. Distinguish repository HEAD from installed release,
   live observations and the experiment's baseline. A compacted recollection
   is a retrieval hint, not evidence.
2. Reconstruct the active claim from §2 and the affected requirements in §3.
   Check its actual consumer and referenced findings. Locate the governing
   source, not only another summary that cites a summary.
3. Check omission as well as error: did an obligation, counterexample,
   unknown, alternative, authority boundary or non-claim vanish? Did bounded
   evidence become general capability, a prototype become promoted, or a
   surface repair displace the native objective? Did the next action change
   without an evidenced reason?
4. When sources disagree, correct tracking explicitly and reopen only the
   affected claim. Do not smooth the conflict by weakening requirements.
   If authoritative state is unavailable, name the uncertainty and recover it;
   do not invent a status or let a stale summary authorize action.

Every substantive reply ends with **Tracking**. Every display or refresh uses
all five labels below, including when a field has not changed:

- **Target:** the stable PoC capability, not a surface-specific substitute.
- **Stage:** the active stage and its bounded claim, with its Atlas claim ID.
- **Evidence/open gaps:** what is established, what is not, and the material
  obligations or counterexamples still due. Prototype evidence stays distinct
  from admitted capability and installed or live evidence.
- **Boundaries:** operative decisions, authority limits and preservation rules.
- **Next/trigger:** the next movement and the evidence or decision warranting it;
  identify any actual blocker instead of implying that work continues unseen.

Before sending, check these fields against the controlling requirements, the
active Atlas claim and its recorded evidence—not the previous Tracking text.
Check for omitted obligations as well as incorrect claims. Keep each field
concise; use precise claim/source references for detail, never compression that
silently drops an unresolved dependency. This is active state, not a turn recap
or a growing second rulebook. Only change standing rules when they actually
change. Missing verification stays missing across replies. No independent test
is claimed when the same builder reviewed it. Presence of all five labels is a
mechanical check, not proof that their contents remain aligned.

At a stage boundary or context recovery, include the checked source revision
and governing IDs in the opening/closure record. During work, new evidence,
changed controls, a widened claim, an unexpected result or a different next
action triggers re-anchoring immediately—not after another long investigation.
Old tracking is explicitly superseded when corrected, never silently carried
forward as corroboration. These checks keep the tracker responsive to discovery
without allowing discovery or compression to rewrite the destination.

Mechanical checks may detect broken references, stale baselines, missing
claim fields and changed controls. They do not prove understanding, semantic
truth or Soul participation. Human review can challenge the source-to-claim
reasoning. Track the tracker against primary sources and omission checks, not
by comparing two favorable summaries or relying on memory to notice drift.
